PharmaVerse AI

Next-Generation B.Pharmacy AI Learning & Clinical Intelligence Platform

An end-to-end intelligent web platform designed for B.Pharmacy students and faculty. Features 8 Semester PCI Curriculum coverage (42 Subjects), AI Medicine & Pill Scanner Pro with ADME pharmacokinetics, AI Tutor, and 1-Click Unit PDF Download Engine.

Project Domain: Pharmaceutical AI & EdTech
Standard: PCI Syllabus (Sem 1 to Sem 8)
Status: Fully Production Ready
Executive Summary

Project Vision & Core Value Proposition

Curriculum Integration

Complete coverage of all 8 semesters (42 B.Pharm subjects) structured strictly according to Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) guidelines.

Clinical AI Vision

Live camera snapshot & computer vision OCR to identify pharmaceutical formulations, active ingredients, IUPAC structure, and ADME pharmacokinetics.

1-Click PDF Notes Engine

Guaranteed client-side Blob PDF generation for 288 Unit PDFs (Unit 1 to Unit 5), including 2M, 5M & 10M university model exam answers.

GPAT / NIPER Prep

Interactive high-yield competitive exam simulator with countdown timers, subject-wise test sets, and instant answer keys.

Feature Deep-Dive

AI Medicine & Pill Scanner Pro

📷 Computer Vision & OCR

Uses HTML5 canvas image processing and live WebRTC camera video stream to capture pill strips and extract printed text imprints.

📊 ADME Pharmacokinetics Profile

Calculates Bioavailability (F%), Elimination Half-Life (t½), Plasma Protein Binding (PPB%), Hepatic Metabolism, and Renal Clearance.

🧪 Chemical Structure & IUPAC

Renders full chemical structure names and heterocyclic ring classifications (e.g. 4-acetamidophenol, Dihydropyridine, PPIs).

⚠️ Drug Interaction Alert

Evaluates critical drug-drug safety alerts (e.g. Warfarin bleeding risk, Ciprofloxacin chelation, Alcohol toxicity).
