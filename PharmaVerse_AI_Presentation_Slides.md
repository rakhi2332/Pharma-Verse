# PharmaVerse AI — Project Presentation Deck

## Slide 1: Title Slide
- **Project Title**: PharmaVerse AI — Next-Generation B.Pharmacy AI Learning & Clinical Intelligence Platform
- **Domain**: Pharmaceutical AI & EdTech
- **Target Audience**: B.Pharmacy Students, Faculty & GPAT Aspirants
- **PCI Curriculum Coverage**: Semester 1 to Semester 8 (42 Core Subjects)

---

## Slide 2: Executive Summary & Project Vision
- **Curriculum Integration**: Complete PCI B.Pharm syllabus integration (Sem 1 to Sem 8).
- **AI Medicine & Pill Scanner Pro**: Computer vision OCR, chemical structure recognition, ADME pharmacokinetics profile ($F\%$, $t_{1/2}$, PPB%), and drug interaction alerts.
- **1-Click Unit PDF Downloader**: Client-side Blob PDF generation for 288 Unit PDFs (Unit 1 to Unit 5).
- **Solution Pharmacy Official Video Hub**: Integrated YouTube video lecture playlists.
- **GPAT & NIPER Exam Portal**: Timed MCQ test simulator with scorecards and mnemonics.

---

## Slide 3: Full-Stack Technical Architecture
- **Frontend Layer**: React 18, Vite 8, TailwindCSS, Framer Motion, HTML5 Canvas Image Resizer.
- **Backend REST API**: Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB Mongoose ODM.
- **Utilities & Engines**: WebRTC Live Camera Stream (`getUserMedia`), Client-Side Blob PDF Downloader, Oxlint Code Validator.

---

## Slide 4: Core System Modules
1. **Semester & Subject Curriculum Explorer**: 8 Semesters, 42 Subjects, Unit 1 to Unit 5 topics.
2. **AI Medicine & Pill Scanner Pro**: Live camera capture, ADME pharmacokinetics, chemical structure.
3. **Interactive In-App PDF Reader**: Unit navigation modal with 1-click downloads.
4. **AI Handwritten Notes & Question Bank**: Notebook-style exam sheets, 2M, 5M & 10M model answers.
5. **Solution Pharmacy Video Hub**: Official YouTube course lectures.
6. **GPAT & NIPER Entrance Portal**: Practice tests, countdown timer, answer rationale.

---

## Slide 5: AI Medicine & Pill Scanner Pro Deep-Dive
- **Live Camera Snapshot**: Real-time WebRTC camera viewfinder for tablet strips and pill imprints.
- **ADME Pharmacokinetics Profile**:
  - Bioavailability ($F\%$)
  - Elimination Half-life ($t_{1/2}$)
  - Plasma Protein Binding ($PPB\%$)
  - Hepatic Metabolism & Renal Clearance
- **IUPAC Chemical Structure & Safety Alerts**: Drug-drug interactions (e.g. Warfarin bleeding, Ciprofloxacin chelation).

---

## Slide 6: PCI Unit Notes & AI Handwritten Exam Sheets
- **✍️ AI Handwritten Exam Sheet Box**: Step-by-step pathways, flowcharts, memory acronyms.
- **❓ 2-Mark Question & Answer Bank**: Crisp 2-line model answers.
- **📝 5-Mark & 10-Mark Model Answers**: Full essay answers with headings, SAR rules, and pharmacopoeial assays.
